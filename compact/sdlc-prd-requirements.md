# sdlc-prd-requirements (compact)

When: Create and refine PRDs, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, assumptions, open questions, constraints, and out-of-scope decisions for software products.

Goal: Create requirements that are specific enough to build and test, without over-specifying implementation.

Steps:
1. Capture goal, background, users, scope, non-goals, and constraints.
2. Write requirements as observable behavior or measurable quality expectations.
3. Tag every assumption, open question, dependency, and decision owner.
4. Connect requirements to BDD examples and product metrics.
5. Revise requirements when design, architecture, or implementation exposes ambiguity.

Never:
- Do not accept vague verbs like improve, optimize, support, or handle unless the observable behavior is…
