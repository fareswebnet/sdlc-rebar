# sdlc-legacy-inventory-registry (compact)

When: Produce a frozen, script-generated registry of everything callable or visible in a legacy system (actions, routes, screens, entities, tables, commands, outbound calls, business rules), with one mandatory disposition per…

Goal: Give the migration a denominator: a registry nobody can quietly shrink, where 100 percent means 100 percent of the old system.

Steps:
1. Inventory each source repository with a script or the framework's own route and schema listing; never replace the inventory with…
2. Enumerate by kind: ACTION, SCREEN, ENTITY, TABLE, COMMAND, JOB, OUTBOUND_CALL (the contract the target must serve), and RULE…
3. Cross-check counts against the framework's own output (route list, ORM metadata, schema dump); explain or fix every gap before…
4. Break write operations into fields: for every create or update, list the form or entity fields and constraints in the line's note…
5. Inventory reference data (lookup tables behind every choice list), implicit constraints (uniqueness, scoping by role or tenant)…
6. Load the result with `python scripts/sdlc_registry.py init <registry.csv> <inventory.csv>`; the tool merges without losing…
7. Assign each line a disposition: OPEN, TO_VERIFY, PORTED (proof plus independent visa), REPLACED (replacement named and existing)…
8. Have the business owner read every ABANDONED and DEFERRED line before the registry is frozen.

Never:
- No target work on a repository until its registry exists, is frozen, and was reviewed.
- Never delete a registry line; a line that disappears from a later inventory is marked, not erased.
- Generated and vendored folders must be ignored by the extractor, but check that no business logic hides…
- Technical fragments (partial templates, AJAX snippets) may be REPLACED up front, but only with the…
