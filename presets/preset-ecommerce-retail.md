# $preset-ecommerce-retail

- Use for storefronts, marketplaces, checkout, inventory, and retail operations.
- Raise depth for `$sdlc-performance-budgets` (Core Web Vitals, checkout latency, peak-load tests), `$sdlc-product-analytics-experimentation`, `$sdlc-observability-reliability`, and `$sdlc-api-versioning-deprecation` for partner integrations.
- Require idempotent payment and order flows, inventory consistency rules, fraud and abuse review, tax and pricing rule matrices, and seasonal peak readiness reviews.
- Rehearse peak events with load tests and a rollback plan before major campaigns.
