# preset-healthtech-regulated

- Use for clinical, patient-data, medical-device-adjacent, or health-insurance products.
- Raise depth for `sdlc-security-privacy-compliance`, `sdlc-accessibility-engineering`, `sdlc-third-party-ip-risk`, and `sdlc-observability-reliability`.
- Require consent and purpose limitation, access audit trails, minimum-necessary data, retention and deletion, and clinical-safety hazard logging with human sign-off.
- Treat AI features that touch care decisions as high risk: evaluation plans, human oversight, and monitoring through `sdlc-ai-product-architecture`.
- State clearly that the suite is not medical, legal, or regulatory advice.
