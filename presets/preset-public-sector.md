# $preset-public-sector

- Use for government, municipal, education, and publicly funded services.
- Raise depth for `$sdlc-accessibility-engineering` (target WCAG 2.2 AA and publish a statement), `$sdlc-security-privacy-compliance`, `$sdlc-documentation-knowledge`, and `$sdlc-third-party-ip-risk`.
- Favor open standards, portability, and exit plans for vendors; record procurement and licensing constraints.
- Require plain-language content, multilingual support where mandated, and long-term archival and retention rules.
- Plan for audits and public records requests: keep decision logs and traceability current.
