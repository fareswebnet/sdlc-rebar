# preset-fintech-regulated

- Use for fintech, payments, lending, financial reporting, KYC, audit-heavy B2B, or regulated workflows.
- Raise depth for `sdlc-security-privacy-compliance`, `sdlc-data-database-architecture`, `sdlc-identity-access-multitenancy`, `sdlc-third-party-ip-risk`, `sdlc-release-production-readiness`, and `sdlc-observability-reliability`.
- Require audit logs, data retention, role separation, access reviews, sensitive workflow BDD, and compliance release checks.
- State clearly that the suite is not legal, accounting, investment, or financial advice.
